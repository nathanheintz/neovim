\section*{Description of Coaching}
Coaching is a co-creative, collaborative alliance between the Coach and the Client with the purpose of supporting and enabling the Client to actualize their personal and professional potential. The coaching relationship is designed to facilitate the discovery of new and more powerful possibilities for the Client, the development of personal goals and strategies that reflect those possibilities, and to support the Client to carry out the actions that will achieve them. The coaching relationship is also designed to support and encourage the Client to (a) face the challenges and barriers to this new reality that may emerge during the course of the coaching relationship, and (b) to transform them into the very path to success.

This Agreement is entered into by and between Coach Nathan Heintz (hereafter "the Coach") and Client \clientfullname{} (hereafter "the Client"), whereby the Coach agrees to provide Coaching Services for the Client following this Agreement in accordance with the following terms:

\subsection*{2. Scheduling \& Cancellation}

The time of the coaching meetings and online location will be determined by Coach and Client based on mutually agreed upon times. The Coach will initiate the scheduled calls and will contact the Client. The Coach will send the Client a calendar invitation which includes meeting details including a zoom link.

The Client agrees that it is the Client's responsibility to notify the Coach of a need to reschedule 48 hours in advance of the scheduled calls/meetings. The Client will forfeit the session if they do not show up for the call. If the Coach needs to reschedule, he will do so at least 48 hours in advance and another session will be provided in lieu of the one missed.
